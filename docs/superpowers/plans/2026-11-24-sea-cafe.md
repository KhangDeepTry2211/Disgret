# Quán San Hô (sea café) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Let a focus session take place in a café under Gret's sea where Gret studies next to an
owner and three sea friends who react kindly to the session.

**Architecture:** A `StudyPlace` preference (profile box) picks the scene of the focus screen. Pure
logic in `lib/data/sea_cafe.dart` decides who sits in the café and who says what at each session
moment; a presentational `SeaCafeScene` draws it; `CafeSessionView` turns focus-session changes
into moments, shows one bubble at a time and offers greetings.

**Tech Stack:** Flutter, Riverpod 3, Hive (profile and sessions boxes), flutter_svg, CustomPainter.

**Spec:** `specs/006-sea-cafe/spec.md`, design `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-24-sea-cafe-design.md`,
ADR-0011.

## Global Constraints

- No network, no new dependency, nothing sent; greetings are ready-made phrases only.
- UI text in `lib/l10n/app_strings.dart` (Vietnamese); colours, sizes and durations in
  `lib/theme/app_theme.dart`; new code comments in English; file header `// 5.6 Quán San Hô`.
- `study_place` missing/unknown → `cafe`; `Session.place` missing/unknown → `sea`.
- Friends: owner Rùa (turtle); seats from Ẩn (crab, Toán), Mochi (octopus, Văn), Sao (starfish,
  Tiếng Anh), Gret (gret, Sinh học), first three not worn. Names Ẩn and Mochi match the Góc của
  Gret catalog.
- Moments: started (owner speaks), returned, breakStarted, breakEnded, halfway; bubble 3 s
  (`AppMotion.speechBubble`); greetings open while focused < 1 minute or on a break.
- Reduced motion (`MediaQuery.disableAnimationsOf`): nothing loops, bubbles without animation.
- Gate: `dart format --set-exit-if-changed lib test`, `flutter analyze`, `flutter test`.

## Review Focus

- Leaving the focus screen and coming back through the banner after the first minute: the owner
  must not greet again (Task 4 test).
- A distraction and a break starting in the same update: exactly one bubble, the break line
  (Task 4 test).
- A student wearing the octopus or the fish: the roster still seats three different friends
  (Task 2 test).
- Café on a 360×640 phone with a long habit name, in light and dark theme: no overflow (Task 3
  test).
- Sessions saved before the feature: read back as `sea` (Task 1 test).
